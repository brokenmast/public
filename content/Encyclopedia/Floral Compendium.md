# Flowered

## Aguria
[Strange Horticulture - Aguria.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXehh69k9soXLThAfY2pgD_dbFUx3Hhja12kVMQnyZjpGeZQhjy-w85cAps82KLW3QqA-ljzCSHDawLdOgLWQmjL_Pyk0Xz3wG_uiS0BZz6ZU_pYYuHBf9Ck25knDcDz1h95zlPEVe8IceWOY-994l6zF9iY?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Aguria
- Scientific Name: Scyphus Spectaculum
- Close Inspection: “Soft Pale Blue Flowers”

Thin green stems with rounded leaves. Presents four grayish-blue flowers that look like cotton balls.

When burned, incense made from the woody stem can enhance psychic abilities, strengthening the clarity of visions and prophecies. Light blue flowers, like rounded tufts of gentle lambswool, characterize this plant.

## Ambrella
[Strange Horticulture - Ambrella.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfRt-SuzN3eXSJsaIqoW-Elg4ufKJuzhCjpE_Fp_hio4jan3vPn3NsmVX0JvSbvwhEQYsbYuRlKvntMUhBpF4O9zj_iIn2Y-VhSiCLO3PLMFK9gRi8hVyeOLpRrkuoBzHZE5nrKMjEDw10bcZIKvj0jnYgW?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Ambrella
- Scientific Name: Sanium Rubra
- Close Inspection: “It has a sweet scent. Like strawberry dipped in honey”

It has a yellow, bulbous root from which four long, rounded leaves come out, their weight pulling them a bit down. Between them, a single five-petal flower, with a composite yellow stamen coming out.

A powerful balm against diseases of the heart. It will not cure once illness has set in but it can ease pain and prolong life. Ambrella has long rounded leaves and a flower that smells of strawberries.

## Bella Nox

[Strange Horticulture - Bella Nox.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeYrk4biGZC_Xq4Z-fdbPjPoMyPby5jbG1tEO-Cre2Hrl44F8lFinb6TREDA1nTbtGMf6B_ogSHLiKEdky98BmBZkEViA1ZtPpu_AYHGD-zMgc-g7bT8mOKeePnBzCxmDgcBapF1P1DQyztinF7gOIGjYo?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Bella Nox
- Scientific Name: Caligo Formosus
- Close Inspection: “Dark purple flowers”.

This plant is made of three very thin stems populated by a lot of small, four-petaled dark purple flowers.

The Moonlight Flower — dark, mysterious and beautiful — has enchanted people for centuries. It is thought to send people mad with obsessive desire, seductively luring them to their deaths like a siren’s call.

## #Burdum

[Strange Horticulture - Burdum.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXcF6gQiQ-qRhiXU2EN5Q_L4WPDez23Aq-IB42hHmsUuedT9LINTfKkze76tVNm8GYqFbehlh5ry3frq3we6xsOTcuktKPuGTO60S1KytgRvpRNcx-VOWGjaIegdZsm674bpCWX4RvHwRry4MwDvcxuumt4m?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Burdum
- Scientific Name: Abula Gilvus
- Close Inspection: “The leaves are strong and leathery”

Four great pointed (but rounded at the tip) leaves surround a singular steam leading to a flower that looks like a real life white daisy.

Thick Burdum leaves make a tasty addition to soup and straws. Superstitious folk won’t allow Burdum into their homes as it is believed to bring bad luck.

## Butterdale

[Strange Horticulture - Butterdale.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfSPQB_MgvuMVDOj7K8ckk-eo-HnJozcvfx7JroZGHArrNybbVpqZCX3dSPsS-i_wqzCUY-g4uAOz4TqGtqtNaoBDu5mUdxGeLoOTiw4WWbpUeswglUr-Tal5Lu0ZE8uaNjHvaPXiCluM-Rmp-_4nwVYone?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Butterdale.
- Scientific Name: Buttyrum Dixerat.
- Close Inspection: “The purple flowers have a light buttery scent”.

You can identify this plant using the Voynich description with the ‘Close Inspection’ - this flower has a buttery scent. Otherwise, you can identify it by its looks: it has thin, dark-green stems out of which come some lobed veined leaves. It has three flowers that, just as the Burdum, look like those of a daisy’s in shape, though purple.

Often found growing in clumps on valley sides. Butterdale has been used for centuries as a cure for all manner of skin rashes. The dark green leaves can be stewed and mulched to form a poultice. The flowers smell buttery to some people.

## Caballia

[Strange Horticulture - Caballia.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeDu5wEzMvdIiul6lklV04rTRFyGcGB7jYHt2Wm932sdWmzSTumWzfIwtyG63Yt4ztGK0xNsq6k_62bN2k6kdjG0vpofdZkPnsszFM12i87XT4PRRl_O28b5Tq_Ywa6FX4U2D_20BLp41RR3C-eOjjKnx0?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Caballia.
- Scientific Name: Arbutum Alacritas.
- Close Inspection: “The flowers are a soft purple with a sweet scent”.

This plant has wide, rugged leaves that grow from its base. From there, thin tall stems grow upwards, ending up on purple six-petal flowers with multiple, white-ended stamens.

A rare plant that is believed to help the dead find peace in the afterlife. Those who can afford it place wreaths made from it at the graves of their loved ones.

Caballia flowers almost always contain six petals. Finding a seven petaled flower is considered to bring good fortune.

## Candlewood

[Strange Horticulture - Candlewood.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXd8qu2g8GBBqJsuEpggCjl7cUPqmQVkl3afIzd1jALW1UoExvloe0HWT2ujPT9SssnCYn0L_dEVjWy7emI3EJ1ONeowfDFPRzrjZDI5gNLHtV_2zZ0LEP_g-x6yzg5uNyfeKWD67SfCCLqcP_vuq6cR5tE?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Candlewood.
- Scientific Name: Lucernum Callidonia.
- Close Inspection: “Bright red flowers grow at the ends of thin stems”

This plant looks like a weed in nature: it has thin leaves that look like grass, as they’re long and grow from the base; while its flowers look like those of a thistle, though fully coloured in red.

Produces a red flower that provides a shielding effect, protecting against all manner of afflictions of the mind. Long thin stems grow among tall leaves.

## Cauldery

[Strange Horticulture - Cauldery.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXe9F6T_fxngYbb3GRlPEtSVamGNwtNWEhA_O3rfgUi8Po4FdqSuZyvrhHcbJWhgIQjqId9Fp7DXkMGn1Q2oogNBOs1WGQw9aTvoCIwPO8OLkXZLVksbYW8SYf5KBSOBcxTzd7n3GNh7Nl0clM-fDliW_2Xq?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Cauldery.
- Scientific Name: Acutus Major.
- Close Inspection: “The flowers are deep blue. Quite beautiful, really”.

This one grows from a ‘tube’ of sorts, out of which five thin curved stems come out. Small and diametrically opposed leaves grow to both sides of each stem, before they end in a five-petaled deep-blue flower that points upwards, along with its stamen.

The uses of Cauldery are not well known. Some say it has applications in dark magic but this author has found neither substance nor specifics to back up those claims. Those who worship the huntress, Arduinna, place the flower as an offering to ensure a successful hunt. It grows from a thin stem with small leaves.

## Clavilium

[Strange Horticulture - Clavilium.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXf4ZAOZR5w-F0wuKL_FjY9-aM6583fX3JmNtVfV99kZ98p-EiG4UPLylFqXYSGrsuhQUWV7Hil2iVxiTWPLtse-mfHklNNPcpfrT8FIiMZ_RCU_9cDSTDajRklBKoWKN7H7IgtY6Lt1r_AEuwHCl4pzHNFR?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Clavilium.
- Scientific Name: Marsilea Clavis.
- Close Inspection: “The flowers are made up of many smaller blue flowers. They smell pleasant”.

It's a plant with several, big heart-shaped green leaves. Between them, two stems with flowers that lead into composite flowers - that is, large inflorescences (lavender-looking, except in color) made up of smaller, four-petal flowers.

According to legend, Clavilium can be used to open any lock, no matter how complex or secure. The heart-shaped leaves have earned this plant the nickname ‘Lovelock.’

## Daisy Dock

[Strange Horticulture - Daisy Dock.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfrQmPMOni7q1xjSgxCb0auDsbEcUjdxswKdv2yXNWs6FRCJQLNGZqJmVtP6WsfAuM37bEHNewBro_BCWf31WgNd17Qogjc39jWF8mCB9R7_w07FE4vVvOcwkfveGRothrmrTxJEF1WrixsGkJyd7FCAw_s?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Daisy Dock.
- Scientific Name: Primula Familiaris.
- Close Inspection: “Blueish purple flowers”.

Though called Daisy Dock, this plant does not look like a real-life daisy at all! Instead, try to identify them by their dark-green tripartite leaves (made of three parts). The flower, meanwhile, is made up of five rounded blueish-purple petals, with three white-ended stamen coming out of the centre.

A simple potion that will grant courage can be brewed from the leaves. It is said that ancient warriors would take it before going into battle.

## Demmel

- Scientific Name: Demmus Splendor.
- Close Inspection: “A bland-smelling flower”.

The Voynich description for this plant puts it best: “This unremarkable flower is related to the common thistle”. And it’s noticeable: look at its weedish-looking serrated leaves, and look at that pair of big thistle-looking aquamarine flowers.

This unremarkable flower is related to the common thistle. Its leaves are occasionally brewed into a tea that is used to calm anxiety.

## Devil’s Nighttrap

[Strange Horticulture - Devils Nighttrap.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfwfsfvG4nGi5CCy2o2lzsI4PDaw2tQoc4jBch5zLGFNbxQ9CPaCIaItxPHTL4le266X6pwQHFPG4xefu-bMFq3KdSARq-chHR0hqc0SdXhmNrpBmgRtyuCydhuIVxEcGnDsaVjaMc85JcVREjcjzQeAJo?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Devil’s Nighttrap.
- Also known as: Corpse’s Friend.
- Scientific Name: Mors Amicus.
- Close Inspection: “It has a soft aroma that reminds me of a pine forest”.

Several thin pointy leaves, of a green color. The flower meanwhile, is a barely open (strong) red bulb, out of which two stamens with yellow ends come out.

This plant is known colloquially as a Corpse’s Friend. If ingested as a seed it will cause a slow agonizing death and the plant will grow out of the body once deceased.

It has a light pleasant scent, not dissimilar to pine needles. Boiling the root in vinegar creates a tonic against fevers and poisons.

## Dranthium

[Strange Horticulture - Dranthium.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXc1e-0uCOLSeMo84i_NEIU9REQP6RySBA-67-yN3B4xFJWkEmJlvgmvVvjYEWMxjU-SNzBblisQvrycPa3Ij3KLQZMqHMDkTeDiHb6uquKUB2Uvb_XOv8nybMev2DilvwIoe7UfZHWLQ4tD5VtUcjmC9gg?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Dranthium.
- Scientific Name: Mens Fortis.
- Close Inspection: “Purple flowers, long thin leaves that bear surprisingly sharp edges”.

This plant, as said under ‘Close Inspection’, has long thin leaves that remind of tall grass. The flowers, meanwhile, are pretty unique, being composed of several bell-shaped, purple smaller flowers.

A plant with powerful mind-altering abilities. Those who inhale smoke from burning its long sharp leaves elevate their awareness to levels otherwise unachievable.

Some know Dranthium as Green-Eye, a name derived from the tell-tale sign of overdose where even the whites of the eyes take on a green tint.

## Elderphinium

[Strange Horticulture - Elderphinium.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeBccuRPfi4nmGiFOlX3OFRVoANB3hcAvXtetOIkLZM6H0lf6e6k5vFrXG6MDjCd8khsBT1OFkFLoiLAxQV1TP0HE1jubyKbxF6z1UxahA2uvjtzta3cCoRwJmN6c1f9QJ4MDZ0If7utqV_6oxG7rXZvjs?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Elderphinium.
- Scientific Name: Maior Corydalis.
- Close Inspection: “It has a strong sweet aroma”.

The Elderphinium stands out on its own: it’s a one-flower plant, with pointed, creased, leaves; with a flower of green petals and lots of purple-ended stamens coming from its center.

The petals of this flower can be brewed to create an antidote to ‘Dendra’s Miasma’- a deadly poison that causes the blood to slowly turn black, eventually resulting in a painful death.

It has an intoxicating scent, similar to that of Solomon’s Sceptre.

Embersoul

Embersoul is thought to have protective properties. It is sometimes given as an offering to various deities in return for sanctuary.Produces flowers with many petals. Watch out for the leaves, they’re razor sharp.

## Eyebright

[https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXf2a92J1RWyeAJDDtYbiQL5fOgRL9EoyYUJuhMUyxXB5jtzkBsIpNbD6Go-zeEGUp5xCCQyIS8stdzia4e7qPOaP99GgZiK2C7jrR0LG3RRMXBp9txMMBFEKi_JltSk2ESMKLtTD-gu1zR3V70di2zls_8B?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXf2a92J1RWyeAJDDtYbiQL5fOgRL9EoyYUJuhMUyxXB5jtzkBsIpNbD6Go-zeEGUp5xCCQyIS8stdzia4e7qPOaP99GgZiK2C7jrR0LG3RRMXBp9txMMBFEKi_JltSk2ESMKLtTD-gu1zR3V70di2zls_8B?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Eyebright.
- Also known as: Loethe.
- Scientific Name: Euphrasia Officinalis.
- Close Inspection: “Those thorns look menacing, I’m staying well clear of them”.

This plant is easily noticeable given that its thick stems contain several, big yellowish thorns. Its leaves are veined and slightly undulated; while the flowers (there are two) are made up of a total of nine strong red petals each, with yellow spotted centers.

Take care not to touch its brightly coloured thorns. They will do no lasting damage but they contain a small amount of venom that will cause a severe pain for several days after.A plant that only grows to the south in warmer climates, where they call it Loethe after a long dead king. Known to improve eyesight at night, in even the darkest places.

The strong smelling bulb of this plant has a powerful soporific effect, making it a common ingredient in sleeping draughts.The Camphry produces a beautiful rounded dark blue cluster made up of many smaller flowers.

## Gilded Dendra

[Strange Horticulture - Gilded Dendra.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdXed-jiWC1CEekpFHvB-DnYHzSyeNxDXd4uB_vs1Dd-Dq8unUMccNa9oBEhrj-4Mb-HCZbEpDP7EB1DpeuoDksmyT-9VKx0uxv8Z-BIbjJmHODn1CxLQAkEigzNLmcxdG7T0pb3RI0BSvMWchCraS-1xI?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

## Grey Sandfire

The flame-like ‘tendrils’ give off a smoky scent, adding to the impression of a roaring fire.

## Harlequin Blue

[Strange Horticulture - Harlequin Blue.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXfeEdgJouTq09lqGyG01nj8Q3Fn8t2qDtXyUd5XrgUK6A-5zjX7TzoLNRZ-HM_W3Q76rWHAF2KeFjaatJ0l0Qet9ZEcOqEXjy-oGul3plKMOdcM4ua64V-rAtPNBckyNsi9Qvscv6ma5LFoXdXKtknnJoBF?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Harlequin Blue.
- Scientific Name: Scurra Caerula.
- Close Inspection: “Blue flowers. Light, airy scent”.

Has two dark green stems that curve downwards towards the top, where a pair of thistle-looking flowers are (one per stem). The latter are blue coloured and completely point downwards.

When the bushy blue flower heads die back, they release seeds with downy pappus which assists in wind dispersal.

Once dried and hardened the stems can be burned as incense. As they burn they let out high-pitches shrieks- widely believed to be the spirits of dead souls trapped within the plant.

This fungus is typically found in dense forests, protruding through the mulch. At a glance it can appear as though a corpse is reaching up from the undergrowth. Said to strengthen resolve and sharpen the mind.

## Glowhorn

[Strange Horticulture - Glowhorn.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXeVYMpDGrPy8ayjhZMmdzipW6db5YeoUK4m6uwrhA3wzW4Fmk971qrmJDSV5fkQ-kH35xZIWne8uFddz8BJDKHYX-2-lxfBOnd_aIXOCKOMx77r_q9QqevvTw50Zz6nH9qQKDuW332sZxO0XcDSxM7zFiKv?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Glowhorn.
- Also known as: Forest Lantern, Pixie-Lamp.
- Scientific Name: Lux Agaricum.
- Close Inspection: “It seems to give off a small amount of light”.

Like the Voynich description says, these mushrooms are phosphorescent: they glow with a tinge of light-green; color expelled by its four conical, white-spotted caps. The Glowhorn, or Forest Lantern or Pixie-Lamp, is phosphorescent, illuminating the trees that it grows under.

Suspicious Travelers avoid walking towards it, fearing that the faint lights will lure them to their demise.

## Sunset Mountcap

[Strange Horticulture - Summer Mountcap.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXe4cP0dYuCNpbMHzHKlpaVIisAYDTh40f5t7CO3-UJRcc16naj2bkCe3tS4rLYjodYFvsgGfocx21y7rgQcT6-YQs6d-Cykbm54ypz_1xTNVkJ-Oq7Dnw2UhUIsTBQbnOhSBp5aJ78HbG85z5dYOEvN84IL?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Sunset Mountcap.
- Also known as: Stinking Mountcap.
- Scientific Name: Solis Occasum.
- Close Inspection: “It smells strongly of soured milk, bad enough to make my eyes water”.

This mushroom’s made of three cream-coloured dome-shaped caps; but what makes it easy to recognise is the set of flies that fly around it, probably attracted by its pungent smell. The Sunset Mountcap is a type of fungus with domed caps. Sometimes called Stinking Mountcap as a result of its unpleasant odor.

It is thought to deter unwanted souls and spirits from entering the home and is often placed in doorways. It is mildly toxic to humans and should not be eaten.

## Wandering Bue

[Strange Horticulture - Wandering Bue.png](https://lh7-rt.googleusercontent.com/docsz/AD_4nXdRVkcp8RIwXfrq2rXK7VRFIj5L6m7d554WSB30MD-Nyc72zlPrHn3aovM2dRgwSzacpEKD0SvWEG9K9IG2sfaw_9Mfbdgu4mF_Irz2VLcIxu4iKUweq0LYuCzLBORQ8-6AvLCpvPp4wSC3gOtAdxwgdr4?key=BHbXaFBwpTU4kw1mSlnucw)

- Name: Wandering Bue.
- Also known as: Finger’s Bane.
- Scientific Name: Bulium Vagus.
- Close Inspection: “Not as spongy as most mushrooms. In fact the undersides are somewhat sharp. Red spores protrude from the caps”.

Five thick stems lead to wide domed caps, all coloured in a cream-like color. On top of the caps, small red protrusions (spores). So called because this fungus can move slowly in the dark. It has been known to cover distances of up to several meters in a single night.

The ‘gills’ on the underside of this mushroom form beautiful flowing lines. They are also quite hard and sharp, earning this plant the nickname ‘Finger’s Bane’.
